## What this proves

1. **The full loop works unattended**: auto-scan → auto-select → real on-chain order → funding collection → risk monitoring → (on exit) auto-rotate. 16.4h with zero manual intervention.
2. **The strategy finds the right market**: in a testnet where funding was negative/zero everywhere, the scanner picked the single deep-premium venue (ZEC) and stayed with it.
3. **The funding cadence and yield model are real**: 23 settlements ≈ 43 min cadence; measured annualized yield ≈ 32.6% matches the theoretical model (~31% at average rate 25.45 micro).
4. **Delta-neutrality works**: spot ledger PnL mirrors the perp move (price 1222→1211 → short perp profitable, spot leg -0.15U unrealized) — the hedge is doing its job.

## Caveats

- Testnet liquidity is thin; the 20U notional is deliberately small (a 4000U order previously found no counterparty). Mainnet would use the real 1000U/4x params.
- Funding rates are not locked in — they decay (ZEC held 30 all night; ETH decayed 40→20 yesterday afternoon). The bot exits when a position stops paying.
- This is testnet evidence of *mechanics*, not a promise of mainnet profitability.
